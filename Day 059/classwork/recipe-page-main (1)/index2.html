Simple Omelette Recipe


An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables or meat.

Preparation time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooked: 5 minutes


Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional fillings


Instructions

  1. Beat the egg:In a bowl, beat the egg with a pinch of salt or pepper, until they are well mixed.
  2. Heat the pan:Place a non-stick frying pan over the medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Cook teh omelette:Once the butter is melted and bubbling, pour it in the egg.
  4. Add fillings(Optional):When the egg begin to set at the edges, but they are still slighty runny in the middle, sprinkle your chosen fillings over one half of the omelette.
  5. Fold and serve:As the omelette contenius to cook, carefully hold one edge and fold it over the fillings.
  6. Enjoy:Serve hot, with additional salt and pepper if needed.
